## 5. Major Divergence: Live Stream Firehose vs Replay

The live shadow session revealed a **critical structural divergence** between isolated historical token replays and live streaming execution:

1. **Re-entry Churn on Dying Tokens**:
   - In replay datasets, tokens had a limited transaction horizon (30-50 txs total), meaning a token was typically traded 1 to 3 times across its lifetime.
   - In live streaming, active tokens generate hundreds of continuous transactions. When V2.3 exited a position via `HARD_SAFETY` or `TRAILING_EXIT`, the position was deleted from `openPositions`.
   - In the very next block, if a small residual buy occurred, `OpportunityScore` temporarily crossed the Tier B entry threshold ($\ge 65$), triggering an immediate re-entry into a dumping chart.
   - Example: Token `Ee8jPH12...` produced **27 separate trades in 2 minutes**. While it made $+0.3058$ SOL on trailing winners, it lost $-0.45$ SOL on 15 rapid re-entry hard stops.
2. **Execution Drag in Fast Memecoins**:
   - In high-frequency chop, 1.5% entry slippage, 1.5% exit slippage, and 0.001 SOL priority gas create a **33% drag** on performance, proving that rapid re-entries severely compound negative expectancy.
