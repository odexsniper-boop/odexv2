## 4. Forensic Conclusions

1. **V2.4 did NOT fail because of bad entry logic or lack of winners.**
   It successfully identified early runners (`DjoqgFKy` +132.5% MFE, `Af8yT6o1` +12.0% MFE, `67uDsCWj` +7.0% MFE).
2. **V2.4 failed solely because it allocated $4\times$ capital ($0.10$ SOL) to deceptive early traps.**
   Those traps scored high on conventional velocity/opportunity indicators but were structurally prone to instant dev dumps.
3. **By decoupling entry conviction from initial size:**
   Entering all unproven tokens with a probe size ($0.025$ SOL) caps maximum loss to $-0.012$ SOL even in the worst $-48\%$ crash, enabling the strategy to maintain positive expectancy ($+0.0025$ to $+0.0188$ SOL).
