## 1. Executive Summary

Following the OOS-5 validation failure (where V2.4 achieved a 75.6% reduction in churn and saved 81.2% in execution losses, but failed profitability gates on uncurated micro-cap decay tokens), V2.5 was commissioned to investigate the following hypothesis:

> *"V2.4 has adequate token-level safety and re-entry control, but lacks a market-regime / launch-quality filter capable of recognizing when the overall micro-cap launch environment is deteriorating."*

Using **Development Data Only** (`replay/raw_txs_expanded_35.json`), this forensic investigation measured rolling cross-token market features, evaluated regime bucketing (`STRONG`, `HEALTHY`, `NEUTRAL`, `WEAK`, `HOSTILE`), decoupled individual token quality from macro market conditions, and performed strict ablation testing.

### Decisive Empirical Finding:
1. **The Hypothesis is Disproven for Broad Macro Filtering**:
   - Broad market regime filters do **not** provide a stable or robust predictive signal for individual pump.fun bonding curve outcomes.
   - Individual token outcomes are **heavily idiosyncratic**: major runners (e.g., `DjoqgFKy`, +0.0248 SOL) emerged during `NEUTRAL` / low-flow market periods, while catastrophic token rugs (e.g., `3hWnWEed`, -0.0574 SOL) occurred during periods where aggregate rolling market flow was actively positive (`HEALTHY`).
   - Implementing a macro market regime filter in `StrategyOrchestratorV2_5` reduced trade count from 17 to 11, but **deteriorated Net P&L from -0.0609 SOL to -0.0641 SOL** and dropped Profit Factor from 0.30 to 0.28 because it blocked small winners while failing to prevent idiosyncratic individual token rugs.
   - Temporal cross-token momentum (prior trade win rate) showed a **negative correlation (-0.059)** with subsequent trade outcomes.
2. **True Operational Bottleneck Identified**:
   - The failure in OOS-5 was **not** caused by entering during bad macro market hours.
   - The true bottleneck is **Initial Token Entry Selectivity** under Tier C probing (Opportunity Scores between 55–60 accepting micro-caps with inadequate organic buyer depth).

## 2. Market Regime Feature Definitions

The research module [`engines/v2/marketRegimeAnalyzerV2_5.js`](file:///c:/Users/Other%20Stores/Desktop/Meme/ODEX%20V2/ko/engines/v2/marketRegimeAnalyzerV2_5.js) computed rolling 30-minute cross-token causal indicators:
1. **Macro Net Flow Ratio**:
   $$\text{NetFlowRatio} = \frac{\sum \text{BuySOL} - \sum \text{SellSOL}}{\sum \text{BuySOL} + \sum \text{SellSOL} + \epsilon}$$
2. **Positive Flow Breadth**:
   $$\text{PositiveBreadth} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{Tokens with } \text{BuySOL} > \text{SellSOL})}{\text{Total Active Tokens in Window}} \times 100$$
3. **Buyer-to-Seller Ratio**:
   $$\text{BuyerSellerRatio} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{Unique Buyers across Window})}{\text{Count}(\text{Unique Sellers across Window})}$$
4. **Composite Regime Score**:
   $$\text{Score} = 0.35 \times \text{FlowScore} + 0.35 \times \text{BreadthScore} + 0.20 \times \text{ParticipationScore} + 0.10 \times \text{RunnerScore}$$

## 8. Root-Cause Diagnosis & Engineering Recommendations

1. **Reject Macro Filtering as a Silver Bullet**:
   - Trying to gate pump.fun micro-cap trading based on 30-minute macro Solana net flow is a category error. Individual memecoins operate as independent attention liquidity pools that frequently decouple from broader market trends.
2. **Focus on Individual Token Entry Selectivity**:
   - In OOS-5 and Dev 35, the primary driver of losses was accepting Tier C probe trades on tokens with Opportunity Scores of 55–60 where buyer count was under 10 wallets and distribution risk was elevated.
   - The next evolutionary step must focus on **Tightening Base Entry Criteria** (e.g., requiring minimum 12 unique buyers and Opportunity Score $\ge 65$) rather than macro regime gating.
3. **Freeze Inviolability**:
   - V1, OOS-1, OOS-2, OOS-3, OOS-4, and OOS-5 remain permanently sealed and untouched.
