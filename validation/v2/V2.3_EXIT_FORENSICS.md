## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis

In V2.2 OOS-3 testing, the strategy was diagnosed as **defensive but unable to monetize micro-excursions**. Micro-runners on pump.fun bonding curves regularly expanded between $+12\%$ and $+38\%$ MFE before liquidity taking or flow exhaustion triggered flow-stops or hard stops, converting high-quality entries into scratched trades or losses.

V2.3 introduced:
1. **Dynamic Adaptive Harvest**:
   - Defensive harvest trigger at $+12\%$ if thesis momentum decelerates.
   - Momentum harvest trigger at $+30\%$ if flow remains robust.
2. **Breakeven Capital Protection**:
   - Once MFE reaches $\ge +12\%$, the stop loss ratchets to $-1.0\%$ below breakeven, preventing runner giveback.
3. **Runner Trailing Tightening**:
   - After a harvest trigger, trailing distance tightens from $15\%$ to $10\%$.
