## 17. Strategic Synthesis: Why Position-Sizing Alone Is Insufficient

The empirical evidence across 49 closed trades in OOS-7 reveals the structural limitation of pure static position sizing:
1. **Sizing Down High Tiers Cuts Both Tails:** In development data, Tier B had 0 wins, so downsizing it appeared to be pure downside defense. In OOS-7, Tier B had genuine winners. Uniformly downsizing Tier B suppressed winner payouts by $-0.411$ SOL while preserving $+0.340$ SOL on losers.
2. **The Underlying Signal Edge is Too Thin:** With a baseline Profit Factor of 1.02 and execution costs of $\sim 1.5\%$ per trade, altering initial position sizes scales dollar magnitudes and reduces drawdown, but **cannot convert a 1.02 gross strategy into an execution-viable system**.
3. **Dynamic Staged Allocation vs. Static Scaling:** To monetize runners without exposing full capital to single-block rugs, position sizing must be **dynamic and two-staged** (probe entry followed by milestone scaling on confirmed survival), rather than static downscaling of entire tiers.
