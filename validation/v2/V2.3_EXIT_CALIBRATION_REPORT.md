## 1. Executive Summary

In V2.2 OOS-3 testing, the strategy proved remarkably defensive (avoiding catastrophic losses and outperforming V1's $-0.1853$ SOL by $+0.1772$ SOL), but finished slightly negative ($-0.0081$ SOL) because it was **unable to monetize micro-excursions**. ## 2. Why V2.2 Failed to Monetize MFE

A rigorous post-OOS diagnostic revealed the structural mechanism causing V2.2's givebacks:

```
[Entry] ───> [Micro-Run: +12% to +38% MFE] ───> [Flow Weakens] ───> [Flow-Stop / Dump]
                     │                                   │
             V2.2: Waits for +40%                 V2.2: Sells at -9.7% / -11%
                   (Never triggers)                     (Gives back +64% MFE!)
```

1. **Unrealistic Harvest Target in Micro-Liquidity**: Pump.fun tokens early in the bonding curve face natural consolidation bands after rapid 20-30% moves as early snipers exit. Setting a mandatory $+40\%$ first harvest threshold meant that healthy, tradeable micro-trends were left exposed to 100% principal risk.
2. **Asymmetric Flow-Stop Sensitivity**: Once buyer acceleration slowed down, the flow-stop triggered indiscriminately, even if the trade was still well above the entry price. Without a prior partial harvest, the entire position was liquidated at the bid.
3. **Absence of Breakeven Ratchet**: Trades that achieved significant MFE ($+12\%$ to $+30\%$) had no mechanical capital protection, allowing them to cycle all the way down to hard safety stops ($-20\%$ to $-40\%$).

## 3. V2.3 Architecture & Calibration Changes

V2.3 implements a disciplined, multi-layered exit framework governed by strict priority rules:

### Priority Hierarchy
```
1. Emergency / Rug Liquidity Drain Override
   └──> 2. Hard Safety Stop (-20% to -40% based on tier)
         └──> 3. Thesis Broken State
               └──> 4. Flow & Liquidity Reversal Failure
                     └──> 5. Breakeven Capital Protection (Arm at >= +12% MFE, Stop at -1.0%)
                           └──> 6. Time Decay Stagnation
                                 └──> 7. Adaptive Profit Harvest:
                                       ├── Defensive Harvest: +12% MFE if momentum decelerating
                                       └── Momentum Harvest: +30% MFE if thesis strong
                                       └──> 8. Runner Trailing Exit (Tightened from 15% to 10% post-harvest)
```

### Core Parameters
- **Defensive Harvest**: $+12\%$ MFE, $50\%$ position size, active when thesis weakens (`DECAYING`, `BROKEN`, or low momentum).
- **Momentum Harvest**: $+30\%$ MFE, $50\%$ position size, active when thesis remains `HEALTHY`.
- **Breakeven Protection**: Armed automatically when MFE reaches $\ge +12.0\%$. Ratchets stop loss to entry price $-1.0\%$ (covering execution slippage/fee buffer).
- **Post-Harvest Trailing Stop**: Trailing distance tightens to $10.0\%$ from the high-water mark once a harvest occurs.

## 7. Known Risks & Degradation Boundaries

1. **Early Profit Clipping on Mega-Runners**: Taking 50% profit at $+12\%$ or $+30\%$ reduces total token inventory for 10x-100x moonshots. This is an intentional design trade-off prioritizing survival, positive expectancy, and drawdown control.
2. **Slippage on High-Volatility Pump.fun Exits**: If a bonding curve suffers a sudden multi-SOL dump, the breakeven ratchet stop ($-1\%$) may execute at a slight deficit due to block-level liquidity gaps.
3. **Regime Sensitivity to Low-Volatility Choppiness**: If the market enters an ultra-low volatility regime where tokens never exceed $+10\%$ MFE, neither the defensive harvest nor breakeven protection will arm, falling back to flow-stop defense.
