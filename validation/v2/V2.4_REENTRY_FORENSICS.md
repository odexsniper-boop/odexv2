# KO V3 — V2.4 RE-ENTRY FORENSICS & OPERATIONAL DIAGNOSTIC

## 1. Executive Summary

During Live Shadow Mode validation of V2.3 (122 seconds on Solana mainnet), the exit monetization ladder succeeded in capturing major runners (+1.0087 SOL across 30 trailing exits, MFE +30% to +136%). However, the session revealed a critical live operational vulnerability: **Re-Entry Churn**.

Without token-level memory or risk budgeting, volatile or dumping tokens triggered repeated entries immediately after catastrophic stops. A single token (`Ee8jPH12`) triggered **27 consecutive entries in 50 seconds**, racking up consecutive `HARD_SAFETY` stop-losses and generating **33% fee drag** on negative gross P&L (-1.1479 SOL market loss, -1.5266 SOL execution loss).

To eliminate this vulnerability without compromising legitimate early runner capture, **V2.4 Token-Level Re-Entry & Anti-Churn Calibration** was engineered and audited.

### 2.2 Root Causes Identified
1. **Zero Memory Between Trades**: V2.3 treated each incoming event independently. The orchestrator had no state tracking whether a token had just stopped out 100ms ago.
2. **Same-Slot / Sub-Second Re-Entry**: In high-frequency bonding curve blocks, multiple buy transactions generated immediate Opportunity Scores $\ge 55$, immediately opening a new position the moment the previous one was closed.
3. **No Risk Budget per Token**: A single dumping token was allowed to consume unlimited entries and capital until the market closed or liquidity vanished.
4. **Execution Fee Compound Drag**: Each probe entry incurred priority fees (~0.000005 SOL) plus slippage (1.5%), converting small fluctuations into substantial cumulative drain.

## 3. V2.4 Architectural Solution: State Machine & Dual Protection

To solve this without naive fixed timers that blind the engine to real runners, V2.4 introduces two coupled architectural components:

### 3.1 Component 1: `ReentryGuardV2_4` (State Machine)
Tracks per-token state transitions:
```
  [ELIGIBLE] ──────(Buy Trigger)─────> [ENTERED]
      ▲                                   │
      │                                   ▼ (Position Exit)
      │                               [EXITED]
      │                                   │
      │                             (Compute Dynamic Cooldown)
      │                                   ▼
      │                               [COOLDOWN]
      │                                   │ (Cooldown Expired)
      │                                   ▼
      │ (Thesis Reset Confirmed)      [REARMING]
      └───────────────────────────────────┤
                                          │ (Failed Reset / Negative Flow)
                                          ▼
                                     [COOLDOWN]
```

#### Dynamic Exit-Reason-Aware Base Cooldowns:
- `HARD_SAFETY` / `EMERGENCY`: **60s** (Strict lockout after severe dump)
- `FLOW_FAILURE` / `LIQUIDITY_FAILURE`: **30s** (Medium reset after order book deterioration)
- `BREAKEVEN_PROTECTION`: **15s** (Short pause after scratch trade)
- `TRAILING_EXIT`: **5s** (Permits runner pullbacks and second legs)
- `DEFAULT`: **30s**

#### Progressive Loss Multiplier:
On consecutive losses ($N > 1$), cooldown scales exponentially:
$$\text{Cooldown} = \text{BaseCooldown} \times 1.5^{N - 1}$$
Two consecutive `HARD_SAFETY` stops result in: $60 \times 1.5 = 90\text{s}$. Three consecutive stops = $135\text{s}$.

#### Thesis Reset Requirement (`REARMING` $\rightarrow$ `ELIGIBLE`):
When cooldown expires, entry is NOT automatic. The token must independently verify:
- 30s Net Flow $> 0$ SOL
- Buy Volume Acceleration $> 0$
- Distribution Risk $< 75$
- Coordination Risk $< 80$

---

### 3.2 Component 2: `TokenExposureManagerV2_4` (Risk Budgeting)
Enforces hard quantitative limits per token:
- **Max Consecutive Losses**: **2** (Token permanently transitions to `TOKEN_LOCKED` after 2 back-to-back losses)
- **Max Lifetime Entries**: **3** (Prevents endless churning on choppy tokens)
- **Max Cumulative Exposure**: **0.30 SOL** (Hard ceiling on cumulative position capital)
- **Max Cumulative Loss**: **0.05 SOL** (Prevents aggregate capital bleed)
